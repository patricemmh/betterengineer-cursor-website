Engineering role

Frontend Engineer

Builds the user-facing layer of web applications - turning designs into fast, accessible, interactive interfaces.

Typical range: $90k–$150k (US) · varies by market

A frontend engineer owns the experience users actually touch. At BetterEngineer, our frontend engineers pair design fluency with AI-assisted workflows to ship polished interfaces faster, without cutting corners on accessibility or performance.

Core skills

ReactTypeScriptCSSAccessibilityPerformance

Responsibilities

  • Translate designs into responsive, accessible UI components.
  • Optimize page load and runtime performance.
  • Collaborate with designers and backend engineers on APIs.
  • Write tests and maintain a component library.

Frequently asked questions

What does a frontend engineer do day to day -
They build and maintain the parts of an application users see and interact with, working closely with design and backend teams.
What skills matter most for a frontend engineer -
Strong JavaScript/TypeScript, a modern framework like React, plus an eye for accessibility and performance.
