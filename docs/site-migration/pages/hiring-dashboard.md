**FAQ question:** Can I invite other team members to the platform? How does team collaboration work?

**FAQ question:** How do I post a job or define what I'm looking for in a candidate?

> Click "Create Job" on your dashboard and fill out the job details, including required skills, responsibilities, and any must-haves or cultural fit markers. The platform will prompt for all critical info, and your dedicated account manager will follow up to clarify details and ensure the right candidates are presented.

**FAQ question:** What candidate actions can my team perform on the platform?

> You can:

- list item: Schedule interviews
- list item: Leave structured feedback after interviews
- list item: Select or reject candidates at every stage

> All feedback syncs with your hiring manager and our talent team, so nothing gets lost.

**FAQ question:** My company needs to ensure compliance and transparency. How does the dashboard help?

> The dashboard was built to support secure, compliant hiring at every step. You can assign supervisors to contractors and track onboarding milestones. All required documentation and approvals are managed in-platform for easy access.

**FAQ question:** What happens once I select a candidate?

> Once you hit "Select," our team takes over to handle all admin: contracts, onboarding, payroll, and compliance. You can track every step and receive updates, giving you full visibility without the paperwork headache.

**FAQ question:** Can I manage current and previous contractors through the platform?

> Yes. You'll see a complete roster of active and inactive contractors, with details on their supervisors and responsibilities. You can assign managers, give performance feedback, and quickly access reports as needed.

**FAQ question:** What if I'm not sure how to use a feature, or something isn't working?

> Your account manager is always just a message away. Use the direct chat on your dashboard for personalized support.

**FAQ question:** Is my company too small (or too large) for the dashboard?

> Our platform works for startups needing their first critical hires, enterprises requiring risk-managed scale, and agencies demanding flexible team augmentation. Your dashboard adapts to your size and workflow.

**FAQ question:** Is my candidate/company data secure?

> Yes. We use industry-standard security and privacy protocols to safeguard your data. Only your assigned platform users and our internal talent team may access your information, never third parties.

**FAQ question:** What's the fastest way to reach support?

> Use the in-platform messaging tool anytime for a direct, prioritized reply from your account manager. For urgent support, email support@betterengineer.com.
