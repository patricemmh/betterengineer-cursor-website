**FAQ 7**

- **question**: What is nearshore outsourcing?
- **answer**:

> Nearshore outsourcing is a model where you partner with professionals in countries that are geographically close to your own, like U.S. companies working with engineers in Latin America. This approach offers several major advantages over traditional outsourcing to distant locations such as Asia or Eastern Europe:
>
> - Time Zone Compatibility
> - Cultural and Language Alignment
> - Travel and Overlap
> - Quality and Retention
> - Regulatory Simplicity
>
> Simply put: Nearshore outsourcing is about getting the cost and scale advantages of global hiring, without the pain of communication barriers or time zone headaches. That's why more U.S. companies, especially those needing ongoing, high-collaboration product work, are switching from traditional offshore to trusted nearshore outsourcing companies like BetterEngineer.

> - You get speed + quality. We match you with pre-vetted engineers in as little as 72 hours.
