### Section 8 — Frequently Asked Questions (6 Q&A)

Section heading (H3 on the live page):

> Frequently Asked Questions

Q1:

> Am I a potential candidate for BetterEngineer?

A1:

> Yes, if you're a senior software engineer with a proven track record of shipping real products, solving complex problems, and working effectively in remote teams. Our ideal candidates have strong technical skills, excellent communication, and at least 5+ years of experience in modern tech stacks. While a CS degree helps, equivalent experience is just as valuable.

Q2:

> Will I need a visa or have to relocate to work with BetterEngineer?

A2:

> No visa needed! All roles at BetterEngineer are remote. You can work from anywhere in Latin America without relocating or acquiring a visa. We specialize in placing engineers directly on distributed teams at top US companies.

Q3:

> What is the screening and interviewing process like?

A3:

> Our process is streamlined to be respectful of your time. After you create a profile, our team reviews your experience. If there's a potential fit, we'll invite you to a short technical and cultural interview. No endless rounds: we focus on understanding your skills, goals, and preferences so we can connect you with the right opportunities fast. For client interviews, we'll prep you ahead of time so you can shine.

Q4:

> Do I need to speak English to work for BetterEngineer?

A4:

> Yes, strong English communication is essential. Since most of our clients are based in the US, engineers must demonstrate Advanced or Fluent proficiency in English. We assess your communication skills during the interview to ensure you're ready to collaborate on distributed teams with clarity and confidence.

Q5:

> How does payment work?

A5:

> You get paid in US dollars, every month, reliably and on time! All your hours are carefully tracked, and you submit a monthly invoice reflecting your work. We process payments swiftly, usually within the first two to five business days of the following month. With flexible and trusted payment options like Wire transfer, ACH, Payoneer, and Husky, you can count on smooth, hassle-free payments every month.

Q6:

> How do I join the BetterEngineer network?

A6:

> Getting started is easy. Just create a profile with your experience and preferences. Our team will reach out if there's a strong match and guide you through the next steps.

## 5. Forms

### 5.1 Live form observed

**There is no form on this page.** This is the single most important finding of the extraction, and it contradicts the initial assumption that the page is form-centric.

Across all four extraction passes, no `<form>` element, no HubSpot form embed, no `hbspt.forms.create()` call, no `hs-form` wrapper class, no `data-form-id` / `data-portal-id` attribute, no `js.hsforms.net` script, no `/_hcms/forms` endpoint, and no newsletter or search form were observed. There is also no cookie/consent banner text and no consent checkbox, because there is nothing on the page to consent to.

The page is a **conversion-to-external-app page**, not a lead-capture page. Every conversion action is an anchor tag pointing off-site to the applicant app:

| CTA label | Destination | Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join Our Talent Network | `https://app.betterengineer.com/sign-up/candidate` | Hero |
| Begin Your Application | `https://app.betterengineer.com/sign-up/candidate` | End of hiring-process section |
| Apply to Join | `https://app.betterengineer.com/sign-up/candidate` | Closing CTA band |
| Sign Up | `https://app.betterengineer.com/sign-up/candidate` | Footer, Careers column |
| Create a profile (in-copy) | `https://app.betterengineer.com/signup` | Hiring-process step 1 reference |

Caveat on method: WebFetch converts the page to markdown before analysis, which strips `<script>` bodies and most non-semantic attributes. A HubSpot form that is injected client-side after page load would not appear in any pass. However, three independent signals argue against a hidden form here: (1) no static form container or placeholder div was returned, (2) all five conversion affordances are ordinary anchors to an external app, and (3) the header "Hire Engineers" CTA points at a *separate* page (`/multi-step-contact-form`), which is where the HubSpot form work actually lives. Treat "no form on `/join`" as high-confidence but re-verify with a raw-HTML view (browser DevTools or view-source) before deleting any form scaffolding from the rebuild plan.

### 5.4 Gap analysis vs the existing BetterEngineer intake-form pattern

The established new-site pattern is a hand-built HTML form posting to the HubSpot Forms API (portal **8679235**) through a Cloudflare Worker proxy with Turnstile, wired by `intake-form-shared.js`, with fields `firstname`, `lastname`, `email`, `message`, a `company_website` honeypot, and hidden `utm_campaign` / `utm_content` / `utm_medium` / `utm_source`.

**Mapping result: nothing to map.** Because `/join` has no form, there are zero live fields to compare, zero fields needing new handling, and zero HubSpot form objects to recreate. `intake-form-shared.js` should **not** be loaded on this page.

This is a good outcome for the migration, and it specifically retires the biggest anticipated risk:

- **File / resume upload: not required.** The pre-extraction concern was that a candidate-facing page would need a resume upload, which the Worker-proxy pattern does not support (HubSpot file uploads require a different multipart flow and a separate File Manager scope). No upload field exists on `/join`. That gap does not need to be closed for this page. Do not build upload support on account of `/join`.

What still needs deliberate handling:

1. **Preserve the external-app CTA contract exactly.** All three buttons plus the footer "Sign Up" must continue to point at `https://app.betterengineer.com/sign-up/candidate` as absolute URLs. Do not root-relativize them, and do not "helpfully" convert them into an on-site form. Converting this page to an on-site HubSpot form would fork the candidate pipeline away from the applicant app and is a product decision, not a migration decision.
2. **Resolve the `/signup` vs `/sign-up/candidate` inconsistency.** The in-copy "Create a profile" reference uses `https://app.betterengineer.com/signup` while every button uses `https://app.betterengineer.com/sign-up/candidate`. One of these is likely a stale redirect. Confirm the canonical path with whoever owns the applicant app, then use it consistently.
3. **UTM propagation is a new opportunity, not an existing behavior.** The intake-form pattern already captures `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` / `utm_content` into hidden fields. This page currently drops all campaign attribution at the app boundary: a candidate arriving from a paid campaign hands the app no UTM context. If attribution across the `www` to `app` handoff is wanted, add a small script that reads the current query string and appends the UTM params to the four sign-up links. Flag this as a decision for the site owner; it is a behavior change, so do not ship it silently.
4. **Turnstile, the honeypot, and the Worker proxy are all irrelevant here.** No spam surface exists on this page. Adding them would be pure overhead.
5. **Manual HubSpot configuration required: none for this page.** No form needs to be created in HubSpot for `/join`. The only HubSpot-side concern is preserving whatever GTM CTA-click tracking currently exists.
