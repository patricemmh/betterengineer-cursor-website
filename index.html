How we score every candidate

  • AI Native

    Our priority

    Designs agents and multi-step pipelines that execute complex work end to end. This is the level we aim to place for teams that want real AI delivery.

  • AI Integrated

    Treats AI as a thinking partner across design, build, and review. Strong judgment on when to trust or reject model output.

  • AI Assisted

    Uses AI for research, code generation, and debugging with clear human oversight on every important step.

  • AI Exploring

    Limited or inconsistent AI use. Solid engineering fundamentals, still building daily AI habits.
